## 1. Research question

Can a structurally later entry architecture reduce Baseline C false starts by requiring:

`initial opening acceleration → pullback to session open → reclaim confirmation → next-minute entry`

while retaining enough right-tail winners to improve after-cost expectancy?

This is NOT a threshold retune of Baseline C. It changes the temporal entry architecture.

## 2. Frozen seed population

The seed population is exactly the Baseline C selected trade set generated by the frozen Baseline C rules:
- full PIT-active Taiwan equity universe;
- 09:01–09:15 causal decision clocks;
- opening acceleration eligibility;
- Top 3 qualified symbols per decision minute;
- max one selection per symbol per session;
- 4-tick anti-chase eligibility at the Baseline C seed timestamp.

Baseline D does not alter the seed-ranking weights, Top-N, acceleration thresholds, or cost assumptions.

## 3. D temporal architecture

For each Baseline C seed at decision clock t0:

1. DO NOT enter at t0.
2. Observe only bars after t0.
3. Require a completed pullback bar whose low is <= session_open.
4. After a pullback has occurred, require a later completed bar whose close:
   - is > session_open; and
   - is > the immediately preceding completed bar high.
5. The first bar satisfying #4 is the reclaim-confirmation bar.
6. Entry occurs only at the open of the NEXT 1-minute bar.
7. Entry must still lie in:
   `(session_open, fourth_legal_tick_above_session_open]`.
8. If the next-minute entry proxy is above the 4th tick, classify as `MISSED_4TICK_NO_CHASE`.
9. If no reclaim-confirmation occurs early enough to allow a causal next-minute entry by 09:15, classify as `NO_RECLAIM_ENTRY`.
10. Same symbol remains limited to one D entry per session.

No alternative pullback tolerance, reclaim threshold, Top-N, lookback, stop, or holding-period search is allowed after outcome visibility.

## 4. Why this is structurally distinct

Baseline C:
`first acceleration → immediate causal entry`

Baseline D:
`first acceleration → wait → pullback to open → reclaim confirmation → next-bar entry`

The information set and entry time are different. D explicitly trades fewer opportunities in exchange for a second market confirmation.
